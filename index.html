Frequently Asked Questions

How do I order online?

Tap Order Online, fill the form and send it on WhatsApp. We then give you a number to pay to.

How do I pay?

Send the total by MoMo to the number shown after you place your order. Then reply paid on WhatsApp.

How long does delivery take?

We deliver as soon as your payment arrives. If it takes longer than you expect, message us on WhatsApp with your order code.

What if I sent money to the wrong number?

Contact your network's customer care straight away and keep your MoMo confirmation message. Then tell us on WhatsApp so we can help you check your order.

Do you ever ask for my PIN?

No. We will never ask for your MoMo PIN, so never share it with anyone.

Are you open at night and on weekends?

Yes, we are open 24/7, every day.

Stay Safe from MoMo Fraud

  • Never share your PIN with anyone.
  • Check every transaction message before you leave.
  • We will never ask for your PIN.
